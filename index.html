Our Mission

Riverbend Yoga Studio is a calm and welcoming place to stretch, build strength, and relax. We teach classes for beginners, busy workers, and older adults.

New to Yoga?

You do not need any experience to start. Our teachers show easy options for every pose, and we have free mats and blocks you can borrow.

Featured Class
